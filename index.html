Clarity

A desktop task manager for people who want more structure than a plain to-do list

Read the docsPricing

Clarity is a desktop task manager built around Kanban boards, with extra tools for people who want more structure than a plain to-do list: saved cross-board views, projects and milestones, a query language, automations, reminders, and two-way sync with Jira, Google Calendar, and Slack.

Automate the busywork

Templates, automations, and column rules handle the repetitive parts so you don’t have to.

Connect your AI

A built-in command-line tool lets AI assistants (like Claude Code) and scripts read and manage your tasks directly — through the same database the app uses, so changes show up instantly.

Clarity, everywhere

Desktop and Android today, with iOS and web on the way — your boards follow you, wherever you work.

Built for power users

Tasks, habits, packing lists, projects, and so much more — Clarity scales past a simple to-do list without getting in your way.
